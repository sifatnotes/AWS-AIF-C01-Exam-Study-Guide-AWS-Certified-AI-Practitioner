# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) Study Guide

Welcome to the ultimate community-driven study guide for earning the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner** credential by passing **Exam AIF-C01**.

Whether you are a business analyst, product manager, project lead, or IT professional looking to validate your understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Generative AI (GenAI) technologies on AWS, this repository provides a practical, structured roadmap to master foundational AI concepts and AWS-native AI services.

---

## 📌 Certification Overview

To earn the **AWS Certified AI Practitioner** badge, candidates must successfully pass one exam:

* **Exam Code:** AIF-C01
* **Exam Title:** AWS Certified AI Practitioner
* **Exam Provider:** Amazon Web Services (AWS) / Pearson VUE
* **Question Count:** 65 questions (50 scored, 15 unscored)
* **Format:** Multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, and matching
* **Passing Score:** 700 / 1000
* **Duration:** 90 minutes
* **Validity:** Certified for 3 years.

---

## 🎯 Who Should Take This Exam?

- **Non-Technical & Business Leaders:** Managers, consultants, and product leads seeking to evaluate AI/ML use cases, costs, and feasibility without writing deep algorithm code.
- **Practitioners & Operations Teams:** Professionals responsible for integrating AWS AI tools (like Amazon Bedrock or Amazon Q) into business processes.
- **Tech Professionals Expanding into AI:** Engineers transitioning into cloud AI frameworks who want to master Responsible AI principles, model selection, and prompt engineering fundamentals.

## 🧠 Detailed Study Notes & Important Concepts

### Domain 1: Fundamentals of AI and ML (20%)
* **Core Terminology:** Defining Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI hierarchies.
* **Machine Learning Types:** Supervised Learning (classification, regression), Unsupervised Learning (clustering), and Reinforcement Learning.
* **Model Training Lifecycle:** Data collection, preprocessing, model selection, evaluation metrics, and basic concepts of overfitting vs. underfitting.

### Domain 2: Fundamentals of GenAI (24%)
* **Foundation Models (FMs) & LLMs:** Understanding transformer architectures, multi-modal FMs, tokenization, and context window constraints.
* **Prompt Engineering Strategies:** Crafting effective prompts using zero-shot, few-shot, and chain-of-thought techniques to optimize model responses.
* **Fine-Tuning vs. RAG:** Differentiating between Retrieval-Augmented Generation (grounding responses with external search data) and full or Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA).

### Domain 3: Applications of Foundation Models (28%)
* **Amazon Bedrock:** Accessing industry-leading FMs via an API, managing guardrails, knowledge bases, and agent orchestration.
* **Amazon SageMaker AI:** Understanding high-level managed ML capabilities, SageMaker JumpStart, and pre-built ML workflows.
* **AWS Pre-Built AI Services:** Overview of specialized services including Amazon Q (developer & business assistant), Amazon Rekognition (vision), Amazon Comprehend (NLP), Amazon Polly/Transcribe (speech), and Amazon Lex (chatbots).

### Domain 4: Guidelines for Responsible AI (14%)
* **Responsible AI Pillars:** Addressing model bias, fairness, explainability, transparency, robustness, and privacy protections.
* **Mitigating Hallucinations:** Implementing Guardrails for Amazon Bedrock, human-in-the-loop validation, and content filters.
* **Environmental Impact:** Understanding sustainability considerations and resource efficiency in training large models.

### Domain 5: Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI Solutions (14%)
* **Data Privacy Boundaries:** Verifying how AWS ensures customer data sent to Amazon Bedrock is not used to train base models.
* **AWS Shared Responsibility Model for AI:** Partitioning responsibilities between AWS (base infrastructure/FMs) and the customer (prompt inputs, fine-tuning data, application access).
* **Access Control:** Utilizing AWS IAM policies, Service Control Policies (SCPs), and encryption (AWS KMS) for AI workloads.
